## 3. Emotion Mapping (EmpatheticDialogues 32 → our 7)

Confirmed by user 2026-10-03 (`docs/dataset_strategy.md` §6.5):

| Ours | ← ED labels |
|---|---|
| joy | joyful, excited, content, proud, hopeful, confident, prepared |
| sad | sad, devastated, lonely, disappointed, guilty, ashamed, nostalgic, embarrassed |
| angry | angry, furious, annoyed, disgusted, jealous |
| fear | afraid, anxious, terrified, apprehensive |
| love | loving, caring, grateful, faithful, trusting, sentimental |
| surprise | surprised, impressed |
| neutral | anticipating (**low confidence** — ED has no "curiosity" label; anticipating ≈ exploration instinct per `docs/emotions_catalog.md` §3) |

## 4. Construction Pipeline

`tools/build_episodes_v3.py` (seed 42, fully reproducible):

1. Pool all ED conversations (train+valid+test, 23,062 after dedup; take each conversation's opening row, `prompt` = situation, `_comma_` → `,`).
2. Map 32 → 7 labels; per-class round-robin sampling across ED sub-labels (diversity), cap 160/class.
3. Stratified 80/20 split at conversation level (no conversation appears in both splits).
4. V/A/D: mean of NRC VAD v1 scores over lowercase alpha tokens, rescaled [0,1]→[-1,1]; fallback to rule-based class centroid when coverage < 0.20 (52 episodes).
5. Response: contract v1.1 program per emotion + ±15% amplitude / ±10% timing jitter, rendered at 50 Hz, stored at 10 Hz; joint limits verified (`quality.limits_ok` = 1120/1120).

## 5. Validation Summary (v3)

| class | train | test | ED sub-labels | mean V | mean A | VAD coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| joy | 128 | 32 | 7 | 0.388 | -0.032 | 0.36 |
| sad | 128 | 32 | 8 | 0.149 | -0.102 | 0.34 |
| angry | 128 | 32 | 5 | 0.115 | -0.027 | 0.32 |
| fear | 128 | 32 | 4 | 0.075 | 0.032 | 0.36 |
| love | 128 | 32 | 5 | 0.397 | -0.135 | 0.34 |
| surprise | 128 | 32 | 2 | 0.334 | 0.031 | 0.33 |
| neutral | 128 | 32 | 1 | 0.305 | -0.091 | 0.36 |

**Known caveat**: NRC VAD sentence-averaging has a positive baseline bias (common English words skew positive), so absolute V values sit above 0 for all classes. The *class ordering* is correct (joy/love > surprise/neutral > sad/angry/fear). Use V/A comparatively, not as absolute ground truth. A per-corpus centering option may be added in v3.1.
